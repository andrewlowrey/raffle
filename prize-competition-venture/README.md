# Prize Competition Venture

Business case and clickable customer journey prototypes for a UK (Great Britain) prize
competition platform.

## Contents

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Business case dashboard — market analysis, financial model, regulatory position, risk register |
| `customer-journey-prototypes.html` | Three annotated clickable journeys |
| `customer-journey-prototypes.js` | Prototype journey definitions and interaction logic |
| `venture.css` | Shared stylesheet |

## Prototypes

Three journeys, chosen for risk rather than visual appeal:

- **A — Paid entry.** The core revenue path.
- **B — Free entry route.** Demonstrates convenience parity under Schedule 2 paragraph 8(1)(b)
  of the Gambling Act 2005 — the condition the whole legal structure depends on.
- **C — Instant win + reveal.** Demonstrates that revealing a result is free and post-payment,
  as required by Schedule 2 paragraph 6.

Each screen is annotated with design intent, commercial risk, or the specific legal provision
it satisfies. Toggle annotations off for a clean walkthrough.
